Grilled Cheese

Description

A Grilled Cheese Sandwich with Bacon.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 4 slices of bacon, cooked
  • butter
  • 1 slice Cheddar cheese
  • 1 slice Pepper Jack cheese

Steps

  1. Spread butter on outside ends of slices of bread
  2. Place the cheese and bacon inside the bread.
  3. Heat stove to medium heat.
  4. Grill one side until golden brown, about 4-5 minutes. Flip and grill the other side.
  5. Enjoy!
